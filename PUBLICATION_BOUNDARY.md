# Publication boundary

This repository was authored as a clean public reference implementation. It is not an export, mirror, or lightly redacted copy of a private operating environment.

## Included

- General governance rules evaluable without private systems.
- Freshly written documentation, schemas, templates, tests, checker code, and fictional fixtures.
- A fictional tutorial project built specifically for this repository.
- Anonymized field reports preserving the failure mechanism and corrective gate.

## Excluded

- Private directory structures and absolute paths.
- Credentials, correspondence, personal data, customer data, and partner data.
- Internal transcripts, private prompts, private review packets, and private handoffs.
- Unpublished technical subject matter and protected implementation details.
- Filed or draft intellectual-property content not independently published or licensed here.
- Claims that require a private artifact for a public reader to verify.

## Fresh-authorship rule

Public material MUST be written into a clean staging tree. A private-vault file MUST NOT be copied into the public repository and then “sanitized.” Private records may be consulted to recover general failure shapes and rules, but the public expression must be newly authored and independently reviewed for leakage.

## Field-report standard

A field report may preserve:

- the initial claim shape;
- the missing or failed gate;
- how the error was detected;
- the correction and preservation behavior;
- the general enforcement rule produced by the event.

It removes names, private paths, unique formulas, counterparties, private timeline identifiers, and details unnecessary to teach the method.

The root `LICENSE` applies the MIT License to material included in this public repository. Separate private artifacts, inventions, data, systems, records, and correspondence omitted from the repository are outside this repository’s licensing and distribution boundary.
